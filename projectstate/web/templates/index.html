How paying works

Call a tool. If there is no credit on your wallet, the result is an x402 PaymentRequired with the amount. Sign the USDC payment, retry the same call with it attached, and the call runs.

One payment buys credit: ${{ pack }} on a session-based connection (covers tens of calls), or ${{ minpay }} minimum per call without a session. Whatever is left stays on your wallet address for the next call.
